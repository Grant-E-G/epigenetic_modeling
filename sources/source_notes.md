# Source and data provenance

The annotated literature review is in [notes/reading_list.md](../notes/reading_list.md). It distinguishes experimental mechanism evidence, association, mathematical precedent and preprints.

Data downloaded on 2026-10-03 into the ignored `data/raw/` directory. Exact bytes are recorded in [SHA256SUMS](../code/results/SHA256SUMS). NCBI download access initially failed in the network sandbox and succeeded with an approved escalation before implementation.

All dataset discovery was read-only. No external accounts, private datasets or communications were used. Publisher citations in the review were checked through publisher pages, indexed primary abstracts or PubMed/PMC. Full-text access was not successful for every paper; mathematical baseline reproduction is limited to the explicitly described Polycomb approximation.

External annotation follow-up: the ESL supplementary workbook was downloaded from the publisher and the valid hexamer-ranking supplement from a bioRxiv DC1 URL after initial 429/browser-challenge responses. Their checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`. `epidrift annotate` validates 31,744 ESL IDs and 225 ranks, then merges hg19 GEO platform sequences/context into ignored `data/derived/human_features.tsv`. Motif scores average forward and reverse-complement ranks. Annotation coverage is recorded in `code/results/annotation_coverage.csv`. These source definitions were fixed before inspecting the matched ESL effect; the exploratory matching choices were added after the initial baseline run.

Continued validation: the [fibroblast multi-omics source paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01852-y) identifies GSE179848 as the paired RNA accession. Its SOFT metadata and 58 MB processed expression matrix were downloaded successfully from NCBI before RNA-related analysis work; frozen hashes are recorded. The `rnaseq_sampleid` metadata maps expression columns `Sample_n` to culture conditions/times. RNA is retained for a future transcriptomic endpoint, not included in the current conclusions. Measured proliferation slowing comes directly from the GSE179847 sample characteristic `population_doubling_time_uhours_per_division`, at the exact DNA sample endpoint times; only finite positive values qualify.

The [original blood study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5557932/) describes 18 monozygotic and 25 dizygotic twin pairs. The downloaded GEO SOFT exposes sex and individual ID, without family/zygosity linkage. No family relationship was guessed from numbering. This corrects the interpretation of earlier person-bootstrap intervals: 86 participants are not 86 independent families. New reports flag this limitation explicitly.

### Six-test public-data extension

- [Busto-Moner et al. direct kinetics](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007195): observed `AllDat` methylated/unmethylated counts, not published fitted rates or simulations. Chr1/chr22 archives and inference convention come from corrected commit `ec46d95b68fad06072702686c248cad55754fc3b` of `Read-Lab-UCI/DNA-methylation-maintenance-kinetics`. Coordinates are retained as supplied; no uncertain genome join is performed. Timing is post-pulse 0/1/4/16 hours, averaged over a one-hour replication pulse or shifted half an hour.
- [EPI-Clone](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09041-8): public M.1–M.3 object from Figshare file 42479346, author pipeline/amplicon controls/class annotation frozen at `0e612a5dffe060316dfc2a25ebce7fcedcf5c952`. Independent LARRY labels and raw mouse3/mouse4 protein UMIs are from GSE282971. The GEO UMI files measure antibodies, not DNA coverage; undigested amplification probabilities in the panel control probe reliability. Two mouse recipients permit shared-clone decomposition, not direct inference of selective death. Human raw reads are restricted; only public mouse data are analyzed.
- [GSE145698](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE145698): full methylated/total count matrix for WT and five DNMT1 mutant-rescue genotypes, three cultures each. Use provided genomic coordinates within the mouse source only. An effective loss parameter represents rate times unknown exposure, not a measured molecular rate.
- [Reactome ECM pathway](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1474244): gene set from the frozen `ReactomePathways.gmt.zip` download (archive timestamp 2026-06-21; retrieved 2026-10-03). Publisher `current` URL is mutable, so its recorded hash is authoritative. Gene/promoter joins use hg19 GPL13534 names/groups from GSE73115 metadata and the established human feature table. No age or RNA outcome is used to choose genes/probes. This proxy does not assert universal fibroblast identity or gene essentiality.

All newly analyzed source bytes are in the main checksum manifest. API trees/metadata JSONs preserve discovery provenance but are not numerical inputs. The pinned Python environment and large converted TSVs live under ignored `data/`; the format helper is versioned and type checked. Its role ends before numerical analysis begins.
