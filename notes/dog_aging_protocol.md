# Dog aging: frozen functional-protection falsification protocol

Frozen 2026-10-09 after GEO sample metadata and file inventories, before reading methylation/coverage matrix values. This is a retrospective published-data test with an analysis contract fixed in this session, not an externally registered preregistration. Published age/LINE1 associations were already known. Metadata show 1,640 samples/894 dogs, including 538 dogs with multiple ages. All numeric results must distinguish the metadata cohort from the actually eligible sample set.

## Claims and stopping rules

**Historical protection prediction:** independently important regions have smaller within-dog methylation departures than comparable control regions. This is not automatically implied by revision 3, which explicitly excludes functional scores from molecular transitions.

**Revision-3 prediction:** independently calibrated local epigenetic fitness effects predict clone expansion/loss conditional on transitions. Require local editing efficacy/effects, longitudinal clone-linked methylation, clone abundance/absolute counts and appropriate division/composition measurements. Bulk PBMC methylation with gene-essentiality proxies cannot adjudicate this mechanism. Report a failed compatibility gate rather than a fitted selection story.

The practical historical claim tested here is at least a **10% reduction in mean noise-adjusted squared annual methylation change** for essential-associated regions. This threshold is an operational minimum effect, not a prediction derived from the model or a universally meaningful biological boundary. Report the continuous estimate and uncertainty, including whether 5% and 20% effects are excluded. An arbitrarily small unspecified protection coefficient cannot be falsified by a finite dataset. Do not call excluding 10% a rejection of every positive effect or of the full model.

An interval wholly below zero protection supports an opposite-direction proxy result; an upper bound below 10% rejects the practical proxy prediction; intervals spanning 10% are inconclusive at that effect size. Apparent support needs positive lower bounds, context balance and reproducibility in withheld dogs. Even such support is association, not causal repair or validated local importance. No support from generic clock accuracy counts toward the unique prediction.

## External annotations and assembly

Use the existing BAGEL CEGv2 core-essential and NEGv1 reference-nonessential lists frozen in the earlier analysis at author commit 53388adbb4fb0931e5c9dda135502be19e4555f0. Transfer through NCBI human/dog orthology, accepting only unique human-to-dog and dog-to-human mappings among the supplied ortholog relations. Do not match by gene-name resemblance. Use independent NCBI RefSeq annotations on **canFam4**, the matrix's reported assembly. Record exact source hashes. These are human cell-culture gene-fitness proxies, not independently demonstrated dog PBMC methylation-sensitive elements.

Two prespecified arms: (A) promoters within 1 kb either side of a reference TSS, with first-visit methylation <=0.3; (B) gene bodies outside all annotated promoter windows, with first-visit methylation >=0.7. Regions must be wholly contained in the relevant window/body, uniquely assignable to one gene; promoter/body overlaps, multiple genes and sex chromosomes are excluded. Collapse transcript annotation within genes. Promoter hypomethylation and gene-body hypermethylation are operational reference states, not inferred necessities. If one arm fails overlap/coverage, retain the failure rather than choosing a new mask. Arms are separate and require simultaneous 97.5% two-sided bootstrap intervals for confirmatory interpretation; 95% intervals are descriptive.

Do not use LINE1 age hits, clock coefficients, disease association, later methylation or expression outcomes to define importance. Conserved sequence alone is not methylation importance. An independently validated local-effect mask, if unavailable, is an explicit unresolved gate and cannot be replaced by this proxy.

## Observation and split rules

Verify processed values are methylated counts or fractions from file contents and author processing before analysis; verify coverage, matrix headers, locus coordinates and sample ID mapping. Later missingness is missing, never zero. Metadata filename is repeated for every sample and is unusable as a join key; use unique LID/PID IDs matched to matrix columns. Report unmatched columns and absent dogs.

Use one first-to-next available visit pair per dog with initial age >=2 years and elapsed interval 0.5–2.5 years. Require >=20 reads in both measurements. Sensitivities are >=10/30 reads and minimum initial age 1/4 years. Initial-age/interval eligibility comes only from metadata. Primary sample partition is dog numeric ID modulo 5: zero is the withheld evaluation set, others are design/training. All visits from a dog stay together. Site/match eligibility uses training first visits; evaluation coverage can determine measurement availability but must not select matches or weights. Require at least 30 evaluation dogs with paired arm summaries and at least 20 distinct essential/control gene pairs; otherwise no confirmatory contrast. Breed-level independence, relatedness and transfer require available genetic/breed information; do not assert them from breed size categories.

## Matching and outcome

Create gene-level essential/control matches using only external annotation and training first visits. Match chromosome, arm and CpG-island overlap class exactly. Match baseline regional mean methylation (caliper 0.05), mean log coverage (caliper 0.5 natural-log units), region length (length ratio <=2), genomic CpG density and GC content (where obtainable; prespecified calipers 0.02 and 0.05 fractions). Report missing molecular context and common support; absent sequence-density annotations block a fully adjusted claim, rather than silently declare adjustment complete. Baseline means must use at least 20 training dogs with >=20 reads. Retain one deterministic nearest-distance region pair per distinct essential/control gene pair, without reusing either gene. Prefer stable genomic-coordinate order for ties. Do not tune matches based on evaluation changes. Report covariate balance and how many loci/genes are excluded. Chromatin/transcription/replication context are absent unless independently acquired; sequence matching cannot eliminate them.

For counts m,n, define p=m/n. Primary regional outcome is

`[(p_later-p_first)^2 - p_first(1-p_first)/(n_first-1) - p_later(1-p_later)/(n_later-1)] / elapsed_years^2`.

This subtracts unbiased independent-binomial sampling variance; it does not remove biological overdispersion, technical correlation, or batch effects. Retain negative adjusted values; truncating them biases the comparison. If counts are not supplied or cannot be verified, fail the count-based outcome gate. The fraction-only/raw-squared result is secondary and cannot substitute silently. Signed change, raw squared change, coverage and missingness are diagnostics. Mean each region-pair contrast equally within each dog and each dog's summary equally across dogs. Protection fraction is `(control_mean-essential_mean)/control_mean`; if adjusted control mean is <=0, report the difference and a failed ratio endpoint.

Dog bootstrap resamples complete paired summaries (10,000 draws, seed 20261009), keeping genomic masks fixed. Also resample whole matched gene pairs and both dogs/pairs to audit sensitivity to genomic support; correlated neighboring genes and relatives may still limit inference. Bootstrap intervals condition on observed annotation/design and do not propagate uncertainty in essentiality/orthology. Report matched-pair count and effective dog count; CpGs/read counts are not independent biological replicates. Assess batch transitions and essential-control differential measurement quality. If batch change is inseparable from elapsed time, causal calendar-time kinetics is not identified.

Estimate conditional power by injecting prespecified 0/5/10/20% proxy protection into dog/pair summary designs and re-running the endpoint. Clearly label conditional simulations and avoid claiming power for an unmeasured local-effect mask. If data/annotation gates fail, an analytic detectable-effect calculation may be reported as planning only; no empirical rejection is licensed.

### Input-format clarification (2026-10-09; before any contrasts)

The processed matrix concatenates unsuffixed `chr_start_end` regions and labeled aggregate rows such as `chr1_282780_284880_ENPP1`. The primary locus universe is the unsuffixed CpG-region table; exclude labeled promoter/transposon aggregates to avoid reusing overlapping CpGs as extra independent loci. Validate all count entries and report aggregate exclusions. Verify unsuffixed coordinates as one-based inclusive by comparing reference CpG motifs at 2,000 initial starts against the zero-based alternative; require >=95% concordance and a fivefold advantage. Convert accepted intervals to zero-based half-open before annotation/sequence joins. Missing BAGEL human gene IDs are excluded explicitly, without symbol substitution. These are source-format/annotation clarifications, not changes chosen from protection outcomes.

### Flank-context correction (2026-10-09; after initial contrast)

The initial implementation calculated GC/CpG density strictly inside each measured interval. At singleton CpGs this gives GC=1 and CpG density=0 because the following G lies outside the one-base interval; it does not meaningfully match local sequence context. Preserve the initial results under `code/results/dog_aging_interval_context/`. Correct sequence context to the measured interval plus 250 bp on either side, independently of outcomes; 250 bp is the source region-finding gap scale. Keep all calipers, arm definitions, splits and endpoints unchanged. All corrected results are explicitly a post-result context audit, not an untouched preregistered test. The source-format and singleton issue must be disclosed even if the correction gives stronger rejection. Neither the initial nor corrected annotation is direct local methylation importance.
