The design specification is [epigenetic_drift_model_spec_v2.md](epigenetic_drift_model_spec_v2.md); preimplementation corrections are in [model_review.md](model_review.md).

## Biological prediction contract (2026-10-03)

User direction: retain the idea only insofar as it yields biologically meaningful, tractable experiments; no commitment to the current mathematical formulation. The goal is to identify functional constraint on epigenetic maintenance/restoration and distinguish it from differential cell/clone representation. Neither methylation stability nor expression correlation alone demonstrates that constraint.

Every proposed mechanistic extension must specify an observable prediction, competing explanation, external functional definition, held-out condition, and a result that would reject it. Freeze these before inspecting the designated outcomes. Use shared parameters to predict intervention/time transfers; condition-specific recalibration is characterization, not validation. A strong empirical predictor is a required comparator; causal interpretation additionally requires discriminating measurements/interventions. Preserve negative results. Do not learn functional weights or an identity threshold from the same outcome used to validate them. Changes motivated by a failed test are new hypotheses and need new test data.

Current narrow hypothesis: among comparably perturbed, initially methylated gene-body loci, external core-fitness annotation predicts faster intrinsic restoration and subsequent expression recovery beyond baseline transcription, sequence/context, and proliferation. This is a hypothesis, not a consequence of gene knockout essentiality: gene essentiality does not imply functional importance of its gene-body methylation. Separate loss resistance from restoration. Treat DNMT1 maintenance, DNMT3A/B de novo restoration, active demethylation, and clone birth/removal as distinct candidate processes; use elapsed divisions where measured rather than silently equating days with division counts.

Selected public-data feasibility pilot: HCT116 pulse/washout methylation GSE51810, paired expression GSE51811, and independently published BAGEL CEGv2/NEGv1 functional reference sets frozen at author commit 53388adbb4fb0931e5c9dda135502be19e4555f0. These fitness sets are broad cell-culture references, not a normal-colon identity program. The publication already establishes DNMT3B-dependent rebound; reproducing that is a positive control, not our novelty claim. Cancer biology does not establish general aging biology.

Before pilot recovery outcomes are analyzed: use WT baseline and day 5 to identify gene-body probes (single annotated gene, BODY, baseline beta >=0.7, baseline-minus-day5 >=0.15); require external core-essential or reference nonessential membership. Match same chromosome and CGI class, baseline beta, induced loss, pre-treatment expression, sequence fidelity rank and CpG density. Balance genes when summarizing multiple probes. Primary endpoint is essential-minus-control recovery fraction at day 42, relative to each locus's induced loss; retain values outside [0,1]. Day 14/24 are early observations; days 54/68 and mutation comparisons are secondary. RNA expression recovery is secondary and does not define the annotation. No biological confidence interval can be inferred from one methylation array per condition/time; genomic resampling quantifies site heterogeneity only. This is a retrospective pilot on a published study, not preregistered independent validation. Lack of matched support or a nonpositive primary contrast stops this functional-annotation hypothesis from justifying a new lab experiment; a different annotation requires an independently motivated new hypothesis.

Candidate experiment: short perturbation plus washout, targeted methylation and expression at matched loci, independent cultures, division/viability tracking, and clone-sensitive or short-window observations. Include a restoration-enzyme intervention/rescue if pilot support justifies it. The assay must distinguish within-lineage restoration from differential expansion. An initial feasibility stage must establish a methylation change with limited cell loss and recoverable growth before any definitive mechanism test. A collaborator-facing design, exclusions, and go/no-go criteria will be recorded with the pilot results.


The recovery pilot has now executed; [the experiment brief](experiment_brief.md) records the results, revised mathematics, candidate coordinates, and staged collaborator request. The corrected original contrast is +0.312 recovery fraction across 122 pairs. Baseline RNA detection QC leaves seven pairs; tighter expression matching leaves two with an inconsistent +0.079 mean contrast. RNA functional recovery has insufficient support. Do not promote the original association to functional biological validation. The next tractable experiment is local functional editing at four candidate regions, with restoration/selection work conditional on a successful functional test. Baseline detection QC and tighter matching are post-result audits, not preregistered validation.

### Public-data stopping test (2026-10-04; frozen before new outcomes)

Defer wet experiments and lab outreach. Complete the acquired human editing/recovery join and replicated mouse enzyme-clearance benchmark, then stop to assess the distinctive hypothesis. These are retrospective analyses with prospectively frozen splits within this session, not registered experiments.

For the independent editing screen, define signed functional cost as inactive-minus-active mean log viability after construct/plate negative-control normalization; collapse wells within supplied batches and report batch consistency. Keep VP64 and DLD1 as checks, not outcome-selected replacements. Editing efficacy is not independently measured for every target: a null screen score cannot establish that a mark is dispensable. Confirm both guide sequences, forward or reverse complement, within the reported region against reference DNA; accept only an unambiguous assembly and an intact hg19-to-hg38 chain block when conversion is necessary. Never replace failed regions with nearby gene-body sites.

For human recovery, use CpGs with >=10 reads at baseline and day 3, region >=3 shared CpGs, WT mean untreated methylation >=0.5 and day-3 absolute loss >=0.15. Freeze the CpG set using these early samples; later insufficient coverage is missing, never zero. Sensitivities: coverage 5/20, baseline 0.3/0.7 and loss 0.10/0.20. Read late outcomes only after these rules and the functional score are fixed. Days 28/40 are evaluation times; whole regions are excluded during regression fitting. Baseline/loss, region CpG density and early recovery are empirical comparators. Require >=12 eligible independent regions for a small ridge comparison; otherwise report a compatibility failure rather than force a model. No gene-specific biological confidence intervals are possible from single recovery cultures. Control day 40 and DNMT knockouts diagnose culture drift and enzyme dependence; they cannot identify selection versus intrinsic restoration.

For mouse amplicons, retain CpGs with >=10 reads at all required Cre measurements and baseline; do not filter on author confidence labels or fitted rates. Fit days 4/8/10/13 in two cultures, exclude the third culture and days 17/29, and rotate all three cultures. Compare initial constant, last training mean, constant reversible CTMC and known decaying de-novo activity (clearance 0.5/day fixed from the publication). Fit parameters by equally weighted culture/time squared error, independent of read depth; evaluate future means equally by CpG/culture and summarize by independent amplicon, not millions of reads. Repeat fixed clearance 0.25/1.0/day without selecting a preferred version from late outcomes. Inspect mock-control trajectories separately. This validates or rejects an intervention-specific forecasting assumption, not the human functional-restoration mechanism.

Stop when these compatibility and held-out checks are complete. A distinctive claim earns continued priority only through incremental prediction from independent local functional consequences; an enzyme-kinetic fit alone does not satisfy that contract. Failed compatibility is inconclusive about the hypothesis but is a reason not to propose a lab experiment from this join. Negative forecasting evidence narrows the tested formulation rather than motivating unrestricted extra parameters.

### Completed stopping assessment (2026-10-04)

The acquired-data analysis is complete: [results](../code/results/broad_validation.md), [scientific stopping decision](experiment_brief.md#computational-stopping-assessment-2026-10-04). Later regional means require >=80% of the fixed early CpGs and >=3 covered CpGs, each at the gate's read threshold; this measurement rule was implemented before late outcomes. The independent local functional score adds no primary held-out late recovery value. After finding near-complete late recovery, exploratory day-6/day-13 forecasts also fail to benefit from the score. Baseline-only, DLD1, VP64 and inactive-editor alternatives are explicitly exploratory and do not replace the primary score. Low-coverage sensitivity yields some small gains that do not reproduce across times/thresholds. No general functional-restoration claim is statistically disproved from this single recovery trajectory.

Replicated mouse and exploratory genome-scale temporal holdouts favor intervention-aware dynamics over constant CTMC, but a simple delayed empirical loss curve is competitive and WT mocks show a transduction transient. These findings narrow the mathematical formulation; they supply no project-specific novelty. Future work must not relabel these familiar dynamics as validation of functional protection. Keep the equations revisable, require independent prediction, and defer experiments: no current target panel satisfies the biological-prediction contract. This completes the present computational phase; there is no lab outreach or spending recommendation.

Audit clarification: human ridge regressions exclude whole regions, but use other regions' outcomes at the evaluated day to calibrate predictions. They test cross-region generalization, not a simultaneous global future-time holdout. Mouse kinetic fits withhold all late time points. No new-condition functional transfer claim has been validated.
