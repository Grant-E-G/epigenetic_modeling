# Exploratory findings

All real-data analyses in this run are exploratory; no prospectively frozen full annotation table or biological threshold exists.

1. Published processed Polycomb means/SDs match the reported age summaries despite the one-cell count discrepancy.
2. Fibroblast donor-averaged mean absolute earliest-to-latest change is about 0.069 beta units; blood ten-year change is about 0.0177. Different tissues, durations, conditions and platforms prevent interpreting this as a comparison of intrinsic cellular error rates.
3. Simple kinetics show small average predictive gains on fixed probe subsets, with replicate-bootstrap intervals spanning zero.
4. Selection and protection yield matched survivor methylation while producing different death and first-passage distributions. Measuring survivor snapshots alone discards information that could identify the mechanism.

5. External ESLs show lower paired absolute change after coarse sequence/context matching in both human datasets. The donor-averaged contrasts are -0.00573 (fibroblast controls) and -0.00161 (blood). This is stability-annotation transfer, not a demonstrated functional-importance effect. Matching and spatial-null sensitivity remain untested.

No residual drift module, functional-essentiality effect, intervention fingerprint or identity-loss discovery has been established.
