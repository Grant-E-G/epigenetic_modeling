## 2026-10-03: continued real-data validation

- Empirical held-out F1 stability forecasts now run on a fixed denser probe subset with individual earlier beta and external sequence/context covariates. ESL improves the mean donor-held-out log-absolute-change MSE in both cohorts at all three fixed ridge settings; the sign survives joint donor/chromosome exclusion. This passes a limited external-stability predictive gate, not independent functional-constraint validation.
- Full-matrix coarse/standard/fine and no-density matching all retain negative ESL-minus-control contrasts. Finer matching reduces the magnitude and overlap: 14,902 fibroblast and 10,524 blood ESLs retain full fine-stratum support. Matching on cohort early beta remains transductive.
- Restricted low-baseline exact-CTMC M2 has small held-out improvements over pooled M1. Fibroblast restoration estimates hit boundaries. Blood signed-rate M2 scores better by allowing **increased away rates** together with faster return. Suppression of away rates is therefore not specifically supported by these fits. Protection/restoration parameter identification fails under this pooling architecture.
- Independent proliferation endpoint pilot joins actual measured doubling time at the DNA endpoints. Only 11 cultures/eight donors qualify. ESL distance gives a small positive held-out prediction gain across fixed penalties; unweighted/combined distance has no consistent benefit. Gain magnitude varies strongly with penalty, and fixed-score bootstrap excludes training uncertainty. This is suggestive and inadequate for biological identity validation.
- Blood cohort participants are 43 twin pairs rather than 86 independent families. GEO lacks family IDs. Existing person-held-out CV/bootstrap may train on co-twins and underestimate uncertainty. Do not invent a family mapping or treat these intervals as family-valid inference.
- Correct fibroblast paired RNA is GSE179848; its metadata and processed matrix were obtained without access problems and checksummed. GSE225172 is mouse blood RNA. RNA assays have not been analyzed in this follow-up; no transcriptomic validation claim is licensed.

## 2026-10-03: six public-data validation tests

- External Reactome ECM gene/promoter weights were frozen before inspecting RNA associations. Strict future RNA pairing retains 33 pairs/four donors; wider baseline timing retains 63 pairs/seven donors. Functional promoter distance does not improve the tested RNA outcomes and sometimes performs worse than matched masks. A favorable stability mask is not a substitute for this failed functional predictive gate.
- Direct observed Repli-BS counts were read from the corrected author commit. Fixed input-index sampling/coverage yields 2,959 CpGs on chr1/chr22. Memoryless kinetic curves beat an early-snapshot constant but underperform a training-pooled constant at both held-out times. No independent biological rate-validation claim follows.
- Public EPI-Clone objects provide independent LARRY labels. Assay zeros confound methylation-sensitive digestion and dropout; independent undigested controls and raw protein UMI library quality are used. Initial complete-marker filtering would select rare cells positive for every marker; final analysis retains zero observed marker counts in nonempty stained libraries. Static probes show clone-associated held-out gain beyond protein covariates. Representation decomposition across two recipients uses shared independent clones and is not an aging/survival experiment.
- Existing DNMT1 mutations alter methylation. Mutation-calibrated CTMC scaling predicts opposite-parity chromosomes approximately as well as an affine model; this does not distinguish mechanisms. Genotype-held-out independent-effect composition for W465A/W796A substantially underperforms mutation-specific calibration; domain crosstalk is required for that transfer rule. The triple-mutant transfer is much closer. Exposure duration is unavailable, so no molecular rate was assigned.
- Independent CTMC means improve future-array RMSE, but nominal 95% intervals miss about 21% of observations under donor-balanced averaging (about 18% under array-balanced averaging). Current predictive uncertainty is undercalibrated. Residual dependence exceeds independent within-donor shuffles, but does not identify a biological latent state/rank.
- Exact three-locus killed-chain recovery with 40% missing calls and 2% call error often confuses endpoint mechanisms. Repeated snapshots alone do not reliably solve it. Independent survival counts help recover selection/combined mechanisms under known baseline killing; nested models and nearly tied scores remain ambiguous. The simulation excludes clone reproduction and unknown context confounding, so does not establish unrestricted biological identifiability.
- All public inputs were obtainable; the human EPI-Clone raw files are controlled access and were not requested or used. Format conversion uses a pinned local Python reader only; all inference is Rust. No new laboratory data or external communications were used.

- Numerical identifiability audit: strict winner labels are unstable under nearly tied held-out scores. With an exploratory 0.001 mean-log-score tolerance, more than one candidate remains competitive in 22/24 bulk, 21/24 repeated-cell and 20/24 survival-augmented runs. Survival improves some rankings but cannot be reported as solving mechanism identification at this sampling depth.
